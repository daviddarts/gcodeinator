        About G-code-Inator

        G-code-Inator converts SVG drawings into G-code for CNC pen plotting and drag knife cutting. Developed and tested on a LowRider 4 CNC running FluidNC, the generated G-code should work on GRBL, grblHAL and other GRBL-compatible controllers.
